# Sales, Pricing & Profitability Analysis Using Excel

## Project Overview

This Excel project analyzes **sales, purchase costs, pricing, and profitability** across products, categories, and customers.

The purpose of the analysis is to go beyond sales figures and understand which areas are contributing to profitability, which are underperforming, and how selling prices compare with purchase costs.

The final workbook combines detailed analysis with an Excel dashboard for clear business performance reporting.

## Business Problem

Sales alone do not provide a complete picture of business performance. Profitability is also influenced by purchase costs, selling prices, and margins.

The key challenge is to identify:

* The most profitable products, categories, and customers
* Underperforming or loss-making areas
* Whether selling prices are profitable compared with purchase costs
* The difference between strong sales performance and strong profitability

## Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales, cost, and profit performance
* Calculate **Gross Margin %** and **Markup %**
* Compare purchase prices with average selling prices
* Evaluate product, category, and customer profitability
* Identify top-performing and loss-making areas
* Review inventory valuation and available cost build-up information
* Present key findings through an Excel dashboard

## Analysis Performed

### Overall Performance

Analysis of Total Quantity Sold, Total Sales, Total Cost, Total Profit, Gross Margin %, and Markup %.

### Product Analysis

Product-level comparison of sales, cost, total profit, markup, and gross margin.

### Category Analysis

Category-level profitability analysis to identify strong contributors and loss-making areas.

### Customer Analysis

Customer-level analysis to understand contribution to overall profitability.

### Price Analysis

Comparison of Purchase Price and Average Selling Price, including Profit per Unit and profitability measures.

### Inventory & Cost Analysis

Review of available stock valuation and cost build-up components.

**Charts**

## Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights the major profit contributors and loss-making areas across products, categories, and customers.

It also demonstrates why **high sales do not always mean high profitability**, making it important to evaluate sales together with purchase cost, selling price, margin, and total profit.
